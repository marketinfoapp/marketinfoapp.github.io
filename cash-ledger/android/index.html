Made for everyday bookkeeping

Just the tools a personal ledger needs.

Stay organized without turning a simple record of your money into a complicated finance system.

◒

See balances together

Review total balance, cash in, cash out, and the amount held in each account category. Hide balances on screen when you want more privacy.

↕

Keep transactions clear

Add notes, edit or remove entries, and filter by transaction type or account category. Transfers keep both sides of a move connected.

▧

Make separate ledgers

Switch between ledger profiles for different purposes. Choose a supported currency or enter your own symbol and code for each profile.

⟳

Reuse regular entries

Save common transactions as templates. Tap one to fill in a new entry, then review its details before saving.

▤

Export what you need

Create a PDF report, export transaction data to CSV, or select transactions to generate a JPEG receipt for your records.

⇄

Sync with Windows nearby

Send or receive the active ledger over Bluetooth with a paired device. Receiving data replaces that profile after you confirm.

⌑

Add an app lock

Use an optional PIN and Android biometrics to gate access to the app. The lock is an access screen; it does not encrypt the ledger database or exported files.

Set it up your way

Choose from 15 interface languages, set a preferred currency, and name each ledger profile so it is easy to find.

Your records, your choices

Personal records, kept personal.

Cash Ledger has no sign-in or developer cloud ledger. Entries are stored on your device. Android system backup may also back up app data when enabled in your device settings.
