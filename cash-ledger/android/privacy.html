Cash Ledger for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026

In brief: Cash Ledger stores ledger entries in the app on your device and does not require a Cash Ledger account. The free version displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. Optional Pro purchases are securely processed via Google Play Billing. If you use Android system backup, export a report, or sync over Bluetooth, copies of your data may be held by the backup service, in files you choose, or on the paired device.

Who this policy applies to

This policy describes the Android version of Cash Ledger, developed by Phildeveloper. It does not cover Android or Google backup services, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, other paired devices, the Google Play Store, or other third-party products. Those services have their own privacy terms and settings.

Information the app handles

Cash Ledger processes information that you enter into a ledger. This can include transaction names or notes, amounts, dates, account categories, ledger profile names, currency and language preferences, and transaction templates. The app does not ask you to create a Cash Ledger account to use these features.

Where information is stored

Ledger profiles, transactions, and templates are stored in an app database on your Android device. App preferences, including display and lock settings, are stored in app-specific preferences. The app itself does not provide a hosted cloud ledger or send ledger entries to a Phildeveloper developer account service.

Android system backup

This app allows Android backup and uses the platform's default backup behavior. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app database and preferences in a system backup, depending on your Android version, device settings, account, and backup service. Android controls when and where that backup is made. Review your device's backup settings and the Android Auto Backup documentation for details.

App lock and biometrics

You can enable an in-app PIN lock and, where available, biometric unlock. The PIN is stored in the app's local preferences. The lock adds an access screen inside Cash Ledger; it does not encrypt the ledger database or exported PDF, CSV, or JPEG files. Protect your Android device and exported files accordingly.

Bluetooth sync

If you choose Bluetooth sync, Cash Ledger transfers the active profile's ledger name, currency, language, transactions, and templates directly to or from the paired nearby device you select. Receiving data replaces the information in the active profile after you confirm; other Android ledger profiles are left as they are. The app uses Bluetooth for this transfer and does not send the sync payload to Phildeveloper.

Exports and receipts

You can export a PDF report or CSV transaction list and generate JPEG receipts from selected transactions. These files are saved or shared through Android features. Exported files may contain personal financial details; you decide where to keep them and who can access them. Delete exported files separately when you no longer need them.

Advertising (Free version)

The free version of Cash Ledger displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and use device data—such as your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, app interaction data, coarse location, and diagnostic logs—to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, enforce frequency capping, and prevent fraud. For more details on how Google handles advertisement data, please refer to Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

In-app purchases (Pro version)

Cash Ledger offers an optional Pro version purchase to unlock additional features and remove top banner ads. All in-app purchase transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Phildeveloper and Cash Ledger do not collect, process, or store your credit card, debit card, bank credentials, or billing address. All payment processing is managed directly by Google Play.

Retention and deletion

Your app data remains on the device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Cash Ledger. Android system backups may remain available under your device or account backup settings, and exported reports remain wherever you saved or shared them. Remove those copies separately if you want them deleted.

Children

Cash Ledger is a general-purpose bookkeeping utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not collect personal information through a developer-operated account service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
