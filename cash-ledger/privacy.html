Cash Ledger for Windows

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

In brief: Cash Ledger is designed to keep your ledger information on your Windows device. The app does not require an account or send ledger records to Phildeveloper. If you choose Bluetooth sync, the ledger is transferred directly between the paired devices you select.

Who this policy applies to

This policy describes the Windows desktop version of Cash Ledger, developed by Phildeveloper. It does not cover the separate Market Info app, the Android version of Cash Ledger, Microsoft Store services, Windows, or other third-party products. Those services have their own privacy terms.

Information the app handles

Cash Ledger processes information that you enter into your ledger, which may include transaction names or notes, amounts, dates, account categories, currency and language preferences, ledger names, and templates. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or a Cash Ledger account to use these features.

Where information is stored

Ledger data is stored as local files in the Cash Ledger application data directory for your Windows user account (normally %APPDATA%\CashLedger). The app also stores local trial state there. If Windows does not provide an AppData location, the app uses a Cash Ledger folder in your user home directory.

When you enable app PIN protection, the PIN verification settings are stored separately and protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). The PIN lock controls access through the app; it does not encrypt the ledger JSON files or exported backups. Protect your Windows account and device accordingly.

Backups and recovery copies

When you export a backup, Cash Ledger writes a JSON file to the location you choose. Recovery copies are created locally before supported restore and incoming-sync operations. These files can contain the full ledger, including transactions, templates, and settings. Anyone who can access those files may be able to read their contents, so store and share them carefully. You are responsible for deciding where exported backups are kept and for deleting them when no longer needed.

Bluetooth sync

If you start Bluetooth sync, Cash Ledger transfers the ledger data, including transactions, templates, and settings, directly to or from the paired device you select. Sync requires nearby devices paired through Windows Bluetooth settings. The receiving ledger is replaced after you confirm the operation. On Windows, a recovery copy is saved before incoming data is applied. Bluetooth transfers are not sent to Phildeveloper by the app.

Microsoft Store and purchases

If you install Cash Ledger or make a purchase through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process information under its own privacy terms. The app can open the Microsoft Store for Pro upgrade flows. Payment details are handled by Microsoft, not collected by Phildeveloper through the app. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Diagnostics, advertising, and analytics

The Windows app does not include a Phildeveloper account service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cloud ledger service. This statement describes the current Windows app and may be updated if its data practices change. Windows and Microsoft Store may collect diagnostic, installation, or purchase information according to your system settings and Microsoft’s policies.

Retention and deletion

Your ledger files remain on your device until you delete them or remove the Cash Ledger application data. Uninstalling the app may not remove these user data files. To remove local app data, close Cash Ledger and delete the Cash Ledger data directory described above. This permanently removes local ledgers, trial state, and recovery copies in that directory. Separately exported backup files must be deleted from the locations where you saved them.

Children

Cash Ledger is a general-purpose bookkeeping utility and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Please review this page periodically.
