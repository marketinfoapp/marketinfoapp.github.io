Made for everyday bookkeeping

Know where your money is going.

Keep the essential details organized without turning a simple ledger into a complicated finance system.

↕

Track transactions

Record income, expenses, and transfers between Cash, Wallet, Bank, Receivable, and Investment accounts.

▤

Use templates

Save frequent transactions and reuse them to fill in the entry form, then review the details before saving.

⇄

Sync with Android

Send a ledger between paired Windows and Android devices over Bluetooth. The receiving ledger is replaced after confirmation; Windows makes a recovery copy before receiving.

▧

Export useful records

Create PDF summaries, export transactions as CSV, make JPEG receipts, and save or restore JSON backups.

◎

Choose your setup

Set the ledger currency and language, create separate ledgers for different purposes, and optionally protect app access with a PIN.

⌘

Keep control of your data

Ledger files and recovery copies are stored on your Windows device. Exported backups are files you choose where to save.

Your records, your choices

Designed to work locally.

Cash Ledger does not require an account. Ledger data is stored on your PC; Bluetooth sync sends it directly to the paired device you select. Read the full policy for storage, security, and deletion details.
